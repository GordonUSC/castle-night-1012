Tonight's Headliner

What to watch for when Ehrlich takes the Palace stage.

a wand that seems to float

Ehrlich "Firechill" Ocampo

A Filipino artist who began in cheer dance, then fell in love with the Leviwand: a stick that seems to float and orbit around him. It grew out of the classic magician's dancing cane, and Ehrlich turned it into a mix of magic, circus and dance. He brought it to America's Got Talent in 2021 (a standing ovation and four yeses), performed with Cirque du Soleil, and is a featured artist in Awakening at Wynn Las Vegas, where he also performs with a rope dart, a martial arts weapon turned into theater.

"Visibility on a stage like this ... means there is space for us." (Ehrlich, FOX5 Las Vegas)

  • Watch the object, not the hands. The trick is that it never seems to be held.
  • It is choreography as much as magic: rhythm, flow, and the music.
  • His favorite moment is the bow, when he smiles and waves at the audience. Wave back.

This is his first week headlining the Castle's big room, and the Castle has not posted his program, so the set itself may be new.

The Code of the Castle

Dress the part

Suit or jacket with dress shirt, tie, dress trousers and dress shoes. No denim, sneakers or athletic wear. Jackets stay on inside.

Bring your ID

Everyone needs a valid photo ID, and the evening is 21 and over.

Keep the mystery

No photos or video inside. Outside and in the lobby is fine, so take the group photo before Open Sesame.

Dinner is part of the night

The Castle asks every guest to stay for dinner, so come hungry. Our table is at 9:00.

Lines are the trick

The Palace is reserved for us. The Close-Up Gallery and the Parlour are first come, first served, so we line up early.

Say hello to Irma

"Hey Irma," a song request, and a tip in her jar. She has been playing for guests for decades.
